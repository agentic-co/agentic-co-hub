The rule that keeps the palette honest: Attest never decorates. If something is green, it passed a gate. The moment green becomes a generic accent, the mark stops meaning anything and the identity is just three bars. This is why links on the blog carry no colour at all.

Semantic layer

Components bind to these, never to a primitive. Swapping ground rebinds this layer and leaves every component untouched.
