One step of an ASOP, and the record that closes it. Between them they render the whole contract: what must be true, what was done, how it was proven, who was allowed to say so.

The component is shaped so the missing piece is visible. An attestation that names only who and when renders as incomplete, which is the point: an identity and a timestamp are not a verdict. The rejected variant also fires when the signer is the executor.
